# ECHO APEX Recovery Preservation Set

ECHO is preserved as the estate's kinetic capability engine.

The following mechanisms are explicitly preserved and strengthened during recovery:

- execution mesh and heterogeneous worker federation;
- durable execution, leases, recovery, checkpoints, receipts, and replay;
- worker isolation, interposition, cancellation, stale-result rejection, and failure-domain containment;
- frontier ingestion and source-driven capability discovery;
- orchestration, resumability, idempotency, routing, and specialist execution;
- trustworthy observability that accelerates the next move rather than becoming the move.

Counterengineering rule:

A control surface that turns uncertainty into inactivity must be redesigned to route uncertainty into a bounded experiment, simulation, disposable worker, comparison harness, or evidence-acquisition task whenever such a path is technically coherent.

ECHO does not exist to move work through gates. It exists to keep powerful work moving, learning, recovering, composing, and advancing.
